Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit playbiomic.com.

1. What Data We Collect

We collect anonymous analytics data to understand how visitors use our website. This includes:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on each page
  • Click interactions
  • Browser and device information

2. Use of Cookies

We use cookies to enable website analytics. You have the option to accept or reject these cookies when you first visit the website.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic. This service may set cookies on your device. Data is anonymized and not used to identify individual users.

You can learn more about Google’s data practices here: Google Privacy & Terms

4. Email Signups

If you sign up to our mailing list, we collect your email address through a third-party provider (EmailOctopus). Your email will only be used to send you updates about the game and will not be shared with third parties.

5. Your Rights

If you are in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To request this, please email us at contactbiomicpeople@gmail.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: contactbiomicpeople@gmail.com.
